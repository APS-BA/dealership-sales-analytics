## The error found in that output

Its Analysis A reported, for FY2023-24:

```
Other             122,881,779
Air Conditioner   103,528,590
Television        124,299,138
Refrigerator      121,339,682
                  -----------
four categories   472,049,189   vs true year total 435,000,673
```

Four categories alone exceeded the full-year total, with three more unlisted.

**Cause.** Invoice-level `GRANDAMOUNT` was attributed to every category present
on the invoice. Since 40–43% of invoices carry multiple lines, this
double-counts. It also scrambled the category ranking, reporting Air
Conditioner as **third-largest and declining −9.5%**, when it is the
**largest category and the only growing one**.

Its Analyses B, C and D were verified as correct and did not need recomputing.

## Exchange 3 — Correction issued

It was given the diagnosis, the corrected basis
(`LINE_REVENUE = TOTAL + CGSTAMT + SGSTAMT + IGSTAMT`), an explanation of why
`GRANDAMOUNT` cannot be used for category splits, and explicit verification
targets to hit — including that Air Conditioner must come out largest in all
three years with FY2024-25 growth of about +7.5%.

## Assessment of the tool

**Strengths.** Preprocessing was accurate on first attempt — row counts,
duplicate detection, invoice grain, flag counts and customer counts all
verified correct. Analyses B, C and D were correct on first attempt and match
our independent computation to four decimal places (e.g. AC price-index
coefficient of variation 0.007210643614021598 against our 0.0072).

**Weaknesses.** It made a conceptual grain error in Analysis A that it did not
self-detect, despite the output being internally inconsistent — the category
parts exceeded the whole, which a reconciliation check would have caught. It
also accepted its own output as final and presented the wrong figures with full
confidence. Its workbook builder proved unreliable under repeated requests.

**Conclusion for method.** The tool accelerated the work substantially but
could not be trusted unverified. Independent recomputation was what caught the
error, and this is why every figure in the report is reproduced by
`code/analysis.py` rather than taken from the platform's output.
